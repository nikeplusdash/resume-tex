Senior Product Designer — Patient Experience
Meridian Care Systems · Seattle, WA (hybrid, 3 days onsite)

About the role
Meridian builds the software 400 clinics use to run their front desk. We are hiring a
Senior Product Designer to own the patient-facing side of that product: scheduling,
intake forms, reminders, and the messaging that ties them together. Roughly 1.2 million
patients touch these flows every month, a large share of them over 65 or on a screen
reader, and the current experience shows its age.

What you will do
- Own the patient scheduling and intake experience end to end, from problem framing
  through shipped interface.
- Run generative and evaluative research directly with patients and clinic staff. We do
  not have a separate research team; designers here talk to users themselves.
- Extend and maintain our design system, and work in the codebase enough to specify
  states, edge cases, and responsive behavior precisely.
- Drive accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA across the products you own, including audit and
  remediation planning.
- Partner with product managers and a squad of four engineers, and present the reasoning
  behind your decisions to clinical stakeholders.

What we are looking for
- 5+ years designing complex, data-dense product interfaces. Healthcare, fintech, or
  another regulated domain is a plus.
- Demonstrated experience running your own usability testing and turning sessions into
  shipped changes.
- Fluency with Figma and with design systems at component-library scale.
- Comfort reading and writing front-end code (React or similar). You will not be shipping
  production features, but you will be reading PRs.
- A portfolio that shows the thinking, not only the pixels.
- Bachelor's degree in design, HCI, or equivalent practical experience.

Compensation: $155,000–$185,000 plus equity.
